In one sentence

The honest summary.

Dipsea is a large catalog of pre-written, pre-recorded erotic audio stories, English-only, built for passive listening. The app requires an account and a subscription to access the full catalog. Caresse is different on two fronts: (1) you listen to real audio demos before paying, without a Caresse account, in English, French or Spanish — so you know exactly what you're getting; (2) every paid session is generated on demand from your profile (names, genders, practices, intensity, setting), and you can use the entire app anonymously. You're not listening to a story written for everyone — you're generating one for you.

The details

Two approaches, two philosophies.

Dipsea chose editorial fiction. Caresse chose personalized generation. The promise isn't the same.

Caresse

Listen first. Decide later.

Before any purchase, you get access to pre-generated audio demos in English, French and Spanish. No sign-up, no account, no email: you hear right away the sound quality, the voice, the atmosphere and the intensity levels. The demos don't speak your name or use your profile, but they faithfully reflect what you're about to experience.

When you start a bespoke session, Caresse builds a scenario from your profile: who you are, what you go by, what you want tonight, the intensity, the atmosphere. The voice names you. It's not a story written for an anonymous audience — it was only ever generated for this session. And the entire app runs anonymously, with purchases through your Apple or Google account.

  • Audio demos playable without an account (EN · FR · ES)
  • Anonymous mode, no Caresse account required
  • Names spoken aloud in the narration
  • Duration varies with the generated session
  • Essentially infinite combinations: genders, practices, accessories, intensity, setting, atmosphere
  • Native couple and solo modes
  • No subscription: you pay per session

Dipsea

The go-to US library of audio stories.

Dipsea offers a catalog of erotic audio stories, written by authors and performed by professional voice actors, in English. The production quality is well regarded. The catalog is updated regularly and spans a wide range of scenarios and tones.

A possible fit if you want a large fiction audio library and English works for you — as far as we can tell, Dipsea has no French or Spanish version. Less of a fit if you want an experience generated for you and your partner, with your names spoken aloud, or if you'd rather not create an account first.

  • Very large catalog of pre-recorded stories
  • Polished audio production, professional voice actors
  • English-only content, no other language available
  • Built for individual fiction listening
  • Subscription: 7-day free trial, then $69.99/year

How to choose

Honestly, who should pick what?

No spin. Here's who each app is best for.

Choose Caresse if you want…

  • to listen to real audio demos before paying, no account required
  • to use anonymous mode without creating a Caresse account
  • the voice to call you and your partner by your actual names
  • an experience generated for you, not a story written for everyone
  • to personalize the intensity, practices and atmosphere
