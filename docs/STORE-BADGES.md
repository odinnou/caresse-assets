# Store badge assets

The Adesira comparison uses the standard black App Store and Google Play badges, served locally to avoid depending on third-party image requests. Store destinations remain in `src/data/stores.json`.

The SVGs are copied without modification from the public collection [rewilok/app-store-badges](https://github.com/rewilok/app-store-badges):

- `public/badges/app-store.svg`: `packages/core/assets/app_store_badge/black/en-US.svg`, blob `01d7e92e819eeb3f93c44c472b1b41ba9c04b8ce`.
- `public/badges/google-play.svg`: `packages/core/assets/google_play_badge/black/en.svg`, blob `0624d223e292744100b40fdbf3b5b5be3dee6877`.

Apple and Google retain their respective trademarks and badge usage terms.
