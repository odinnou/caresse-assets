The detail

Two ways to experience intimate audio.

Caresse · Generated audio

A session created for you.

Caresse generates guided audio fiction for one adult or an adult couple. Your names, preferences, intensity and atmosphere shape the session, which is narrated by a synthetic voice.

The download and demos are free. They let you hear the format before spending credits to generate a personalised session. Demos do not use your names and are not generated from your profile.

  • Solo and duo modes
  • Audio in English, French and Spanish
  • Your names in personalised sessions
  • Intensity, practices and atmosphere based on your settings
  • Guest mode, with no Caresse account to create
  • Credits for generation, no subscription

Bloom Stories · Editorial catalogue

Human voices and several audio formats.

Bloom distinguishes multi-voice stories, audio roleplays, intimacy guides, audiobooks and relaxation content. Its guides cover both solo exploration and couple experiences, so the service is not limited to passive story listening.

The website offers public samples; a free account provides selected audios, and Premium unlocks the full catalogue. Bloom says its stories are made without AI and that Bloom Chat has been retired. Audio generation using your own names is not advertised in the consulted offering.

  • Stories and guides for solo or couple use
  • Human writing, voice acting and production advertised
  • Stories in English, French, Spanish and German
  • Selected free audio with an account; subscription for the full catalogue

Frequently asked questions

Caresse vs Bloom Stories: your questions.

Is there a free Bloom Stories alternative?

Caresse offers a free download and demos without a Caresse account; personalised sessions use paid credits. Bloom also offers public samples and selected free audios with an account. Neither provides its entire offering for free.

Does Bloom Stories have French and Spanish audio?

Yes. Bloom advertises stories in English, French, Spanish and German. Catalogue availability varies by language; its Spanish page currently says it is not producing new Spanish stories. Caresse offers demos and audio generation in English, French and Spanish.

Does Bloom Stories have guides for couples?

Yes. Bloom Guides include guided intimacy for solo exploration or couple experiences. Caresse differs through sessions generated around your names and preferences, not exclusive access to couple guidance.

Does Bloom Stories use AI like Caresse?

Bloom advertises human-made stories and says it retired Bloom Chat, its former AI feature. Caresse uses AI to generate fiction and a synthetic voice to narrate it. These are different approaches with different strengths and limitations.

Are Caresse’s personalised sessions free?

No. The download and audio demos are free. Generating a personalised session uses paid credits. Caresse does not require a subscription; the optional advanced-settings unlock is a separate purchase.

Do I need to create a Caresse account?

No. You can use guest mode without creating a Caresse account. Purchases require your Apple or Google account with the relevant store. Guest mode does not make the transaction anonymous to the store.
