The process

An experience generated for you, in 4 steps

No catalog to browse. Every session is composed on demand, from your choices.

Step 01

Tell us about your session

Solo or duo, you choose your mode and set up your profile with a simple alias. You set the setting, the mood you're after, the intensity, and any accessories or practices you'd like included. Nothing is mandatory: every setting stays optional and adjustable.

Step 02

AI composes your scenario

From your answers, Caresse generates a unique scenario — never a pre-written text pulled from a catalog. Your names (or aliases), your preferences and the flow you chose are woven directly into the narration, without going into the technical details of the generation pipeline.

Step 03

The voice comes to life

The scenario is turned into audio narration by a natural-sounding voice, paired with ambient sound chosen to deepen the immersion. The result is built to be listened to, eyes closed, without needing to look at a screen.

Step 04

Listen, solo or together

You're notified as soon as your session is ready, usually within minutes. For solo sessions, headphones are best for full immersion. For duo sessions, your phone speaker works, but a Bluetooth speaker is even better for a shared moment. The experience stays strictly private: it is never public, never shared between users.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know

Is every Caresse session really unique?

Yes. Every session is generated on demand from your choices (participants, preferences, intensity, mood): it is never a pre-recorded audio file played back identically.

Does the AI really write the whole scenario?

Yes, the scenario is generated by AI from your preferences, then turned into audio narration. There is no library of fixed scripts that Caresse pulls from.

Is Caresse available on iPhone?

Yes, Caresse is available for free on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

Is my data private?

Yes. Your sessions are never public or shared between users. An alias is enough to create your profile: your real data stays yours.

Can couples generate a session together?

Yes, Caresse has a native duo mode: both profiles and names are woven into the generated scenario, for an experience built as a shared moment.

Which languages does Caresse support?

Caresse is available in English, French and Spanish.
