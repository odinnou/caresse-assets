Frequently asked questions

Before choosing your audio.

What is the difference between Pura Sensa and Caresse?

Pura Sensa presents a library of guided audio sessions. Caresse generates fiction from your names, solo or duo mode and preferences. Both offer intimate audio; their creation and payment models differ.

Is Caresse a Pura Sensa alternative without a subscription?

Yes. Downloading Caresse and its demos are free. Personalized sessions use paid credits, without a subscription. The optional advanced-preferences unlock is a separate purchase.

Does Pura Sensa also offer free access?

Yes. A free tier is advertised, with additional features in Premium. Caresse also offers free demos; its personalized generation remains paid.

Can I use Caresse alone or as a couple?

Yes. Configure one profile in solo mode or two in duo mode. Each person chooses preferences freely and can refuse a suggestion or stop at any time.

Which languages can Caresse generate audio in?

French, English, Spanish and Portuguese. Published demos are in French, English and Spanish. The Portuguese interface is available on Android; the iOS update is awaiting approval.

Does the buyer remain anonymous to Caresse?

Yes. Anonymous mode does not require a Caresse account, and Caresse does not know the buyer’s identity. Purchases are processed through your Apple or Google account and its payment method.
