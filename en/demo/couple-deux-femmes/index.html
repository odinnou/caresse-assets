A session written for two women from the start

Most audio catalogues write one straight scenario and swap the names around. Here the session is built for two women from the start: the gestures, the pacing and the way roles are shared all follow from that. You can hear it within the first minutes, and it is exactly what is missing elsewhere.

What comes after this first phase

This audio is not a montage: it is the complete first phase of a session. The following phases continue in the app. This demo is fixed, with preset names, setting and intensity; in the app, the session is generated on demand with your own names, and no two sessions play out identically.
