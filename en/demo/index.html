Important

How this differs from a real session.

Caresse is not a catalogue of audio files. The first phases available on the dedicated pages are fixed recordings; in the app, the experience is built for you at the moment you ask for it.

Listening pages

The demo

  • Pre-generated once, served identically to everyone
  • Fixed names: James, Emma, Maya
  • A complete, fixed first phase of a little over four minutes
  • One location, one ambience, one intensity level
  • There to let you hear the voice, the tone and the mechanics

In the app

Your session

  • Generated on demand, different every time
  • Your first names, spoken inside the narration
  • A whole experience, usually 25 to 35 minutes depending on what it generates
  • Location, sound ambience, tone, practices and accessories set by you
  • Intensity level dialled in, and boundaries respected
  • Solo or duo, with the couple's dynamic taken into account

In practice: two people who configure the same location, the same ambience and the same intensity do not get the same session. The script, the arc and the transitions are written on the fly by the AI. That is why a pre-recorded catalogue cannot produce the same result: it would have to record every combination of names, locations, practices and intensities.

Frequently asked

Your questions about the demos.

Do I need an account to listen to a Caresse demo?

No. The demos on this page play straight in your browser: no account, no email, no download. Inside the app, usage stays anonymous end to end, payment included.

What is the difference between these demos and a real Caresse session?

These first phases are pre-generated: they were produced once and are served identically to everyone. In the app, Caresse generates the experience for you on demand, from your first names, solo or duo mode, the location, the sound ambience, the intensity level, and the practices and accessories you select. Two sessions configured the same way do not produce the same script or the same arc.

How long are the demos, and a real experience?

The first phases on the dedicated pages run a little over four minutes: long enough to hear the voice, the ambience and the intensity level, without giving away a whole experience. The experiences they are taken from run 26 minutes for the gentle discovery, 32 minutes for the couple and 29 minutes for the solo. In the app, expect 25 to 35 minutes as a rule: length is not a setting, it depends on what Caresse comes up with on the day.

Are the demos available in French and Spanish?

Yes. This page has a French version and a Spanish version, with the same scenarios recorded in each language and names adapted accordingly: James & Emma in English, Mathieu & Clara in French, Álvaro & Marta in Spanish.

Do these demos contain explicit content?

The first demo is set to the app's softest register; the other two are set to its most direct language level. They are guided fiction, audio only, with no visual content whatsoever. Adults 18+ only, with headphones, somewhere you will not be interrupted.

Can we listen to a couples demo from a single phone?

Yes. The couples demo is a single narration addressed to both partners: one speaker, or a shared pair of headphones, is enough. In the app, the session says both first names and alternates between guiding one partner and the other.
