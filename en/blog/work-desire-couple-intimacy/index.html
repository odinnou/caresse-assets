01 · Two busy days

Two full days. Who plans what comes next?

Meetings, different shifts, travel and personal projects can fill your days in many ways, with or without children. Once together, you may not feel like choosing another idea, setting the mood and planning what comes next.

That mismatch alone does not define your relationship. Tiredness, circumstances and health can also play a part. This is about making room for a moment both people want, whatever your genders, jobs or living arrangements.

02 · What research says

Stress, desire and work: understanding the evidence

Research to inform the conversation, without making intimacy a productivity tool.

01

Stress can play a part

A clinical review published in 2024 describes links between stress and women’s sexual function [1]. Stress can affect sexuality, but it cannot explain every change in desire.

This review concerns women’s sexual function and stress broadly. It does not justify applying its findings to all partners or all working situations.

02

Does sex improve performance at work?

A two-week daily diary study of married employees found an association between sexual activity the previous evening and positive mood, job satisfaction and engagement the next day [2].

It does not demonstrate higher objective productivity, an effect specific to orgasm or greater entrepreneurial success. This is observational research: association alone cannot establish causation. It does not test Caresse. Pleasure does not need to earn its place by improving work.

05 · Common questions

Your questions, simply.

Is this only for couples with children?

No. Demanding jobs, travel or personal projects can fill your days. Guided audio together is for adult couples, with or without children.

Does one partner have to lead?

The voice suggests the sequence for both partners. You choose preferences together, and each person remains free to follow a suggestion, skip it or stop.

Is guided audio proven to reduce stress?

The studies cited do not test Caresse. They do not demonstrate that its guidance reduces stress or improves desire. Caresse offers an experience to share, without a guaranteed outcome.
