Guide · First listen · Adults 18+

Your first intimate audio experience together, without pressure.

By Studio Mokuton · 24 October 2026

Curious about an intimate experience guided by a voice? This guide welcomes all adult couples, whatever each person’s gender or sexual orientation. From your first demo to choosing preferences, here are some practical ways to get started together.

Download now

Free demos · No Caresse account · Personalized sessions with paid credits, no subscription.

1. Share the idea

Choose a moment when you can talk without needing to start the experience immediately. Present it as something you are curious about sharing, rather than a problem in your relationship that needs fixing.

Describe what appeals to you: the voice, the surprise of a story or choosing an atmosphere together. A demo gives you a concrete feel for the format.

“Would you like to listen to a demo together and see if we like the voice and atmosphere?”

2. Understand what you will hear

Caresse offers guided fiction narrated by a synthetic voice. A demo is pregenerated audio with fixed names and preferences. It lets you discover the voice and tone without buying a session.

During the demo, notice the voice, the pace and how much space the narration takes. Your impressions will help you choose the atmosphere of your own session.

3. Agree on preferences before generation

To create a session, choose duo mode, names or nicknames and preferences you both enjoy. Every activity requires each person’s free, mutual consent throughout the experience. Consent can be withdrawn at any time; app settings do not replace it.

To understand how this differs from a demo, see how intimate audio is personalized. Generation uses your choices but does not guarantee every phrase or an exact duration.

  • What tone appeals to you tonight: calm, romantic or more intense?
  • Which suggestions do you want to leave out?
  • Which names or nicknames would you like to hear in the narration?

4. Set up your first listen

A romantic date night at home can begin with a conversation and a demo. A comfortable spot and a phone with a speaker are enough to discover the voice together.

For a personalized session, check your setup and the displayed cost before generating it with a credit. Once the audio is ready, set the volume so everyone can hear the narration comfortably.

5. Share your impressions

What did you enjoy: the voice, the pace, the atmosphere, the surprise? Comparing impressions helps you discover what you enjoy sharing.

That conversation can inspire your next session: another atmosphere, a different intensity or new preferences chosen together.

Your questions, answered.

Do we need experience with intimate audio?

No. A free demo lets you discover the voice, pace and style of narration before creating your own session.

Does agreeing before playback cover the whole session?

No. Each person’s consent must be freely given and present throughout the experience, for every activity. It can be withdrawn at any time, regardless of the preferences selected in the app.

Is the first personalized session free?

Demos are free. A session created from your profiles uses a paid credit, with no subscription.

Do I need a Caresse account to buy credits?

No. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse without creating a Caresse account. Purchases still require your Apple or Google account and its payment method on the store you use.

Which audio languages are available?

Caresse generates audio sessions in French, English, Spanish and Portuguese. Demos are in French, English and Spanish. The Portuguese interface is available on Android; the iOS update is awaiting approval.
