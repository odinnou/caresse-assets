Stay with the moment, rather than reading the next step.

A couples massage is a moment of touch and shared attention. A calm atmosphere and gestures you choose together set the tone for your experience.

Caresse brings a voice to this moment. Duo mode and your preferences shape a guided story, with your names or nicknames woven into the narration.

For a first experience, start with an atmosphere you both enjoy. Duo mode, your chosen names and your selected preferences shape your session.

The voice can use your chosen names or nicknames in a generated narrative. Duration depends on the content created; you cannot set an exact number of minutes.

Prepare your massage experience in three steps

01

Download the app and listen to a free demo. Demos introduce the format; they are not a massage session personalized with your names.

Select duo mode, enter the names you want to use and choose sensual massage. Review the mood, intensity and other options before continuing.

03

Create, then listen

Generation uses a paid credit. Once the audio is ready, get comfortable and let the voice accompany your time together.

A moment that feels like you

Every couple is welcome

Caresse welcomes all adult couples, whatever each person’s gender or sexual orientation. Your shared interests shape the experience.

Your conversation still matters

Every touch and every activity require the free, mutual consent of everyone taking part. That agreement matters throughout your time together and can be withdrawn at any point.

Fiction, rather than professional training

Caresse creates an intimate narrative experience. It does not replace professional massage training or promise therapeutic results.

Your questions, answered.

Do we need massage skills to try it?

You do not need prior experience to discover the format. Caresse offers an audio story built around sensual massage; it is not a massage training course.

Do we need to buy accessories?

This setup requires no special accessories. Select app options that match what you have available and want to use together.

Is this a free massage demo?

The app and demos are free to download and try, but a session created from your names and preferences uses a paid credit. Existing demos have fixed configurations and do not promise this particular scenario.

Do I need a Caresse account to buy credits?

No. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse without creating a Caresse account. Purchases still require your Apple or Google account and its payment method on the store you use.

Which audio languages are available?

Caresse generates audio sessions in French, English, Spanish and Portuguese. Demos are in French, English and Spanish. The Portuguese interface is available on Android; the iOS update is awaiting approval.
