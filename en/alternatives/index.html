Frequently asked questions

Your landscape questions.

Which Pura Sensa alternative creates personalized audio without a subscription?

Caresse generates audio fiction from your names and preferences, solo or as a duo. Demos are free; generation uses paid credits, without a subscription. Pura Sensa presents a session library and a Premium tier.

Can I buy sessions in anonymous mode?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse and buy sessions without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and require an Apple or Google account on the store you use, with its payment method. Anonymous mode applies to your use of Caresse, not to the transaction with the store.

Which Melba, Coelle or Dipsea alternative offers real demos without an account?

Caresse offers free audio demos in English, French and Spanish without creating a Caresse account. Personalised generation uses paid credits. Other services also offer samples: Bloom, for example, has public samples and selected free audios with an account.

What makes Caresse unique among these alternatives?

Caresse combines free demos without a Caresse account, sessions generated around your names and preferences, a synthetic voice and credits instead of a subscription. Femtasy and Bloom offer catalogues to browse; Bloom also advertises couple guides and human production. Choose based on the format you want.

Is Caresse cheaper than competing subscriptions?

It depends on how often you use the service, the credits you buy and the available offers. Caresse uses credits for personalised sessions, with no subscription; advanced settings have an optional unlock. A subscription catalogue can suit frequent listening. Compare the prices and terms shown in your country.

Which alternative should I pick if I want English content?

Dipsea, Femtasy and Bloom Stories advertise English audio catalogues. Bloom also offers French and Spanish stories; Femtasy advertises French and German offerings too. Caresse offers demos and personalised audio generation in English, French and Spanish. Choose between a catalogue and generated sessions, as well as subscription and credit-based pricing.

Is there a pay-per-session alternative to Caresse?

Caresse uses credits to generate sessions, with no required subscription. Catalogues such as Femtasy and Bloom offer subscriptions and different ways to try their content. This page does not cover the whole market or claim that Caresse is the only service with one-off purchases.

Is there a Femtasy alternative without a subscription?

Caresse does not require a subscription: demos are free, and personalised sessions use credits. That model can suit occasional use, but it does not mean every session is free.

Does Bloom Stories have guides for couples?

Yes. Bloom Guides include guided intimacy for solo exploration or couple experiences. Caresse differs through sessions generated around your names and preferences, not exclusive access to couple guidance.

How does a game like Desire or Spicer differ from Caresse?

Desire uses scored dares and quizzes; Spicer compares answers to show shared preferences. Caresse offers guided audio fiction. Desire and Spicer also advertise AI: the format, not just the presence of AI, is the difference.

Does Caresse offer Portuguese audio?

Yes, the app creates Portuguese audio. The Portuguese interface is available on Android; the iOS update is awaiting approval. Demos published on the website are in French, English and Spanish, not a Portuguese demonstration.
