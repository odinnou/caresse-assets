Frequently asked questions

Your landscape questions.

Can I buy sessions in anonymous mode?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse and buy sessions without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and require an Apple or Google account on the store you use, with its payment method. Anonymous mode applies to your use of Caresse, not to the transaction with the store.

Which Melba, Coelle or Dipsea alternative offers real demos without an account?

Caresse. Free audio demos in English, French and Spanish, playable immediately — no sign-up, no email, no account. The whole app runs anonymously, with purchases through your Apple or Google account. Among the apps compared on this page, only Melt also lets you start without an account, but in text: for audio, Caresse is the only one that offers it.

What makes Caresse unique among these alternatives?

Three structural differentiators. (1) Audio demos without account creation, in English, French and Spanish — competitors require sign-up to discover. (2) 100% anonymous use, with purchases through your Apple or Google account — among the apps compared here, no other one offers it. (3) Scenario generated uniquely for the two of you every session, with your names, precisely configured (intensity, practices, setting, atmosphere) — the other audio apps compared here run on a pre-recorded catalog.

Is Caresse cheaper than competing subscriptions?

Caresse is pay-per-session, not subscription. Depending on how often you use it, it's often cheaper: you don't pay a yearly subscription for a service you'd use 4 or 5 times a year. Among the apps compared here, every other one runs on a subscription (Coelle $49.99/year, Dipsea $69.99/year, Melba $119.99/year, InTheMoment £7.99/month on Pro), pricing recorded on 17 August 2026 from US App Store listings and official sites.

Which alternative should I pick if I want English content?

Most alternatives are English-first: Coelle, Dipsea, Ferly, Bloom Stories, InTheMoment. Caresse runs native English (and French, and Spanish) within the same app, and you can listen to demos before any sign-up. Dipsea offers a large English catalog of erotic audio stories, Coelle focuses on guided intimacy. Among the apps compared here, Caresse stands out by combining no-account audio demos, a unique scenario every session with your names, and pay-per-session pricing.

Is there a pay-per-session alternative to Caresse?

Among the apps compared on this page, Caresse is the only one offering pay-per-session: Melba, Coelle, Dipsea, Ferly, Bloom Stories, InTheMoment, MagicWave, Melt and Bonded all run on a subscription model (verified 17 August 2026). We don't claim to have surveyed the entire market.
