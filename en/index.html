Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know

Can I buy sessions in anonymous mode?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse and buy sessions without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and require an Apple or Google account on the store you use, with its payment method. Anonymous mode applies to your use of Caresse, not to the transaction with the store.

What is Caresse?

Caresse is an AI-guided intimacy app for couples and solo exploration. It generates immersive audio experiences in real time, personalized to your desires, mood and boundaries. There is no explicit visual content: everything rests on a guiding voice and your imagination.

Do I need an account to try it?

No. A demo experience is available the first time you open the app, with no sign-up. If you go further, an anonymous identifier is enough: a first name or pseudonym of your choice, with no real identity required.

How much does Caresse cost?

Downloading is free and includes demos. After that you pay per session, from €2 — there is no subscription. An optional one-time unlock gives access to more personalization options.

How is this different from an audio catalogue?

A catalogue replays existing recordings. Caresse writes and generates each session at the moment you start it: two sessions with the same settings will never be identical. In return, because the content is generative, slight inconsistencies remain possible.

Which languages is Caresse available in?

The app offers experiences in English, French and Spanish, demo included.

Can it be used both solo and as a couple?

Both. Solo and duo modes are available, and personalization adapts to the mode you choose, including the first names used in the narration. Caresse is for adults only.
