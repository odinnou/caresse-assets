Frequently asked questions

What you want to know

Does Caresse record my voice?

No. Caresse does not capture or record your voice. The voices you hear are generated through text-to-speech (TTS) from the scenario's text, not from a recording of you.

Are my experiences public or shared?

No. Every generated experience is private and accessible only from your account. Nothing is published, shared between users, or made visible to third parties.

Do I need an identifiable account to use Caresse?

No. Caresse works with an anonymous identifier and a first name or alias of your choice. No email address or phone number is required to use the app or make a purchase.

What does Caresse do with my data when I generate an experience?

Session settings are sent in pseudonymized form to AI and text-to-speech providers to generate the audio. No data that could directly identify you is shared with these providers.

Can I delete my data permanently?

Yes. You can request the complete deletion of your account at any time. This permanently erases your experiences, audio files and profile settings, in line with your right to erasure (GDPR).

Where is my data hosted?

On Microsoft Azure servers located in Europe, with encrypted HTTPS/TLS communications between the app and the servers.

Does Caresse sell my data to advertisers?

No. No personal data is sold to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes. Caresse does not use advertising cookies or tracking trackers.

Is Caresse restricted to adults?

Yes. Caresse is restricted to people aged 18 and over, and relies on participants' consent at every step of configuring a session.
