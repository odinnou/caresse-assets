Frequently asked questions

Pricing, made clear.

Is Caresse free?

Downloading Caresse and listening to audio demos are free. Personalized sessions generated on demand use paid credits. An optional personalization unlock is sold separately.

How much does a Caresse session cost?

Individual credit and pack prices are shown in the app in your store’s currency before purchase. One credit is used when you start generating a personalized session.

Is there a subscription or automatic renewal?

No. Buy credits when you want to create sessions. The optional personalization unlock is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Does the unlock include unlimited sessions?

No. It opens additional personalization options. Session generation still uses credits; any credit included in an offer is specified in the app.

Can I buy without a Caresse account?

Yes. Anonymous mode does not require a Caresse account. Payment through the App Store or Google Play still uses your Apple or Google account and its payment method.

Can I try it in English, French or Spanish?

Yes. Caresse offers demos and sessions in English, French and Spanish, for solo or couple use.
