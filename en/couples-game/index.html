A two-player game, in audio

A naughty couples game that doesn't rely on cards.

Most naughty games for couples work the same way: a deck of cards, dice or a list of dares, with questions and challenges written in advance. The content is identical for everyone, and once you've been through the deck, the surprise runs out. Caresse starts from the same idea of an intimate game for two, but replaces the material with an audio session generated on demand.

In practice, each of you sets your own preferences separately: the tone, the mood, the practices that appeal to you and the ones you'd rather avoid. Those choices are combined, but neither of you knows the scenario in advance — not even whoever started the session. That's what preserves the element of surprise in a naughty game for two, without either partner having to write or suggest anything out loud.

Once the setup is done, a voice takes over and guides the session from start to finish. There's nothing to read, nothing to improvise and no turns to keep track of: you listen, and you follow at your own pace. For many couples, that's what makes this format easier to approach than a game where you have to put your own desires into words in front of your partner.

Every session is also different. Because the scenario is generated the moment you start it rather than drawn from a finite list, two sessions set up the same way will never produce the same result. An intimate couples game that doesn't wear out after a few evenings, and that stays free to try with a discovery session, with no account to create.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know

Can we try this couples game for free?

Yes. Caresse is free to download on iPhone and Android, with audio demos available without a Caresse account. Sessions generated from your preferences then use paid credits, without a subscription.

Is this a downloadable game or a browser game?

Caresse is an app for iPhone and Android. Set your preferences and create sessions in the app. This website explains how it works; the download button opens the App Store or Google Play directly.

Can I buy sessions in anonymous mode?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse and buy sessions without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and require an Apple or Google account on the store you use, with its payment method. Anonymous mode applies to your use of Caresse, not to the transaction with the store.

How is Caresse a naughty couples game?

Caresse follows the same principle as classic naughty couples games — each of you sets your own preferences, and the surprise comes from what the other chose — but replaces cards and a board with AI that generates a tailored audio session. It's a different format: no fixed rules, no score, a new experience generated every round.

Do we need to buy a physical game as well?

No. Caresse is an app, free to download, with no cards or accessories to buy. Everything happens in the app: setup together, generation, listening.

Is it a good fit if we've never tried an intimate couples game?

Yes. Caresse always starts gently and lets each of you set your own limits before the session even begins. Because a voice does the guiding, neither of you has to put your desires into words out loud, which makes this kind of naughty game for two easier to approach the first time. There's no obligation to go further than what you configured.

Can we play it repeatedly without getting bored?

Yes — that's the whole point of the format. Unlike a card game with a fixed number of questions or dares, every Caresse session is generated on demand.

What's the difference with a classic couples card game?

A card game offers a fixed set of pre-written questions or dares, which you eventually run through. Caresse generates a new session every time based on your current preferences, with your names woven into the audio narration.
