A two-player game, in audio

A naughty couples game that doesn't rely on cards.

Looking for a couples game on your phone? With Caresse, you set up your moment in the app, then a voice guides you. No cards to read or turns to manage while listening: the narration sets the pace, and you can pause or skip any suggestion.

To create your session, choose names or nicknames, a mood, an intensity and the practices you want to include or exclude. AI then generates an audio scenario from those choices. Generated content can vary or contain inconsistencies, so keep your boundaries and comfort as your guide.

An example for your first evening: listen to a free demo in the app to discover the voice. For a personalized session, then choose duo mode, a romantic mood and a gentle intensity. Creating the session uses a paid credit, with no subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you want to know

Can we try this couples game for free?

Yes. Caresse is free to download on iPhone and Android, with audio demos available without a Caresse account. Sessions generated from your preferences then use paid credits, without a subscription.

Is this a downloadable game or a browser game?

Caresse is an app for iPhone and Android. Set your preferences and create sessions in the app. This website explains how it works; the download button opens the App Store or Google Play directly.

Can I buy sessions in anonymous mode?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse and buy sessions without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and require an Apple or Google account on the store you use, with its payment method. Anonymous mode applies to your use of Caresse, not to the transaction with the store.

How is Caresse a naughty couples game?

Caresse offers an intimate experience for two, guided by a voice in the app. Choose your preferences and boundaries, then AI creates a personalized audio scenario using your chosen names. There are no cards or scores: listen, and remain free to follow, skip a suggestion or stop.

Do we need to buy a physical game as well?

No. Caresse is an app, free to download, with no cards or accessories to buy. Everything happens in the app: setup together, generation, listening.

Is it a good fit if we've never tried an intimate couples game?

Start by listening to a free demo in the app. If you want to create a session, choose a romantic mood and a gentle intensity together, then set your preferences and boundaries. Generation uses a paid credit. Every suggestion is optional and you can stop at any time.

Can we play it repeatedly without getting bored?

Yes — that's the whole point of the format. Unlike a card game with a fixed number of questions or dares, every Caresse session is generated on demand.

What's the difference with a classic couples card game?

A card game offers a fixed set of pre-written questions or dares, which you eventually run through. Caresse generates a new session every time based on your current preferences, with your names woven into the audio narration.
