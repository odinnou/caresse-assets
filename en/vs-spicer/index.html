Two formats

Discuss preferences or listen to fiction?

Caresse

Audio at the centre of the moment.

Caresse creates a guided audio fiction in the app from your preferences. A synthetic voice carries the narration; you remain free to pause, skip a suggestion or stop.

This website does not generate sessions. Demos are pre-generated examples; sessions created in the app are paid.

  • A guided audio fiction, without reading cards during the session.
  • A solo or duo format, narrated by a synthetic voice.
  • Credits instead of a subscription.

Spicer

Questions to discover common ground.

Spicer uses answers to show shared interests. Each partner takes part on their own phone, linked by a code. The format centres on questions, dares and conversation.

The publisher advertises multilingual questions and dares, plus Spicer AI. We do not describe Spicer as an app without AI or free access.

  • Explore questions with yes, maybe or no answers.
  • See shared preferences and discuss them.
  • Link partners by code, including between iOS and Android.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you choose.

Does Caresse match preferences like Spicer?

No. Spicer compares answers to show shared interests. Caresse creates guided audio fiction from session preferences. It does not advertise a matching system or partner chat.

Is Spicer free?

The official website advertises a free base and optional paid advanced question packs. Also check the options and terms in your store and in the app.

Does Spicer have multilingual content and AI?

Yes. Its official website advertises questions and dares in English, German, Russian, French, Portuguese, Spanish and Dutch. Spicer AI is described as suggesting activities from a couple’s matches.

Which Spicer alternative offers audio guidance?

Caresse offers fiction guided by a synthetic voice, solo or as a couple. Downloading and demos are free; creating sessions in the app uses paid credits.

Are Caresse’s personalised sessions free?

No. Downloading the app and listening to demos are free. Creating a session in the app uses a paid credit, with no subscription. The optional advanced-options unlock is separate.

Do I need to create a Caresse account?

Guest mode lets you use Caresse without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through your Apple or Google account and its payment method: the store transaction is not anonymous.
