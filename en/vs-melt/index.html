The detail

Two approaches, two philosophies.

Melt generates erotic text. Caresse generates audio. Same personalization philosophy, radically different medium.

Caresse

A personalized audio scenario, in your names.

Before any purchase, you have access to free audio demos, in French, English and Spanish. No sign-up, no account, no email: you immediately hear the sound quality, the voice grain, the atmosphere and the intensity levels. Demos don't carry your name and don't use your profile, but they give a faithful picture of what you'll experience.

When you launch a custom session, Caresse builds a scenario from your profile: who you are, what you're called, what draws you tonight, the time you have, the intensity level, the atmosphere. The voice names you. The scenario looks like no other, because it was written only for that session. And the entire app works anonymously — with purchases through your Apple or Google account. Each generated experience is then available for life.

Melt Stories

Personalized erotic text, no account needed.

Melt Stories creates personalized erotic fiction for couples and solo readers. You choose a mood, scene, characters and intensity, then read, continue, branch or edit the generated story. No account is needed to start, and several full stories are free.

It's a good option if you want interactive text at your own reading pace. Frequent readers can subscribe for unlimited generation. It's less suited if you want a screen-free audio session or want to hear your chosen names spoken aloud.

Frequently asked questions

Caresse vs Melt — your questions.

Can I buy sessions in anonymous mode?

Yes. Anonymous mode lets you use Caresse and buy sessions without creating a Caresse account. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play and require an Apple or Google account on the store you use, with its payment method. Anonymous mode applies to your use of Caresse, not to the transaction with the store.

Is Melt Stories free?

Melt lets you start in the browser without creating an account and offers several full stories for free. Frequent readers can subscribe for unlimited generation, though its public website does not display the subscription price. Caresse publishes its pay-per-session prices up front — €1.99, €5.49 or €7.99 — and its audio demos play free in the app without an account.

Is Caresse a good Melt Stories alternative?

Yes if you like the personalized premise but want audio instead of text. Caresse generates a guided 25–35 minute session, speaks your chosen names and works in English, French and Spanish — and you can hear exactly what that sounds like before paying anything, with no account.

Do Caresse and Melt require an account?

Neither requires an account to start. Melt lets you begin a story without sign-up. Caresse does not require a Caresse account, even to buy sessions, and offers free audio demos in the app so you can hear the quality before buying anything.

What is the real difference between Caresse and Melt?

The medium. Melt generates erotic text you read. Caresse generates audio you listen to — a synthesized voice speaks your names and narrates a 25–35 min scenario. Same AI personalization logic, radically different experience.

Caresse and Melt: audio or text, in which languages?

Caresse generates audio in French, English and Spanish. Melt publishes text experiences in English, French, German, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. The meaningful difference is therefore audio versus text, not simply language availability.

How much does Caresse cost?

Caresse displays its prices clearly: €1.99 for 1 audio experience of 25–35 min, €5.49 for 3, €7.99 for 5. Each generated experience is available for life. Melt offers several stories free and a subscription for unlimited generation, but does not display the subscription price publicly.

Can you really compare Caresse and Melt?

Not directly — they are different mediums. Melt makes you read a generated erotic story. Caresse makes you listen to a scenario narrated by a voice that speaks your name. Eyes-closed, screen-free vs literary reading experience. Both AI-personalized, both without a mandatory account. The free Caresse audio demos let you hear the difference before buying.

How many different scenarios can Caresse generate?

The combinations are virtually infinite. Each profile combines participants' genders, names, practices, accessories, intensity level, dirty-talk level, location and atmosphere. You never listen to the same thing twice.
