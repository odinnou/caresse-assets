The detail

Useful before. Impractical during.

Both adapt to your answers. What separates them is when they help: Bonded waits for a reply at every step, Caresse asks for nothing once the session has started.

Caresse

From the first spark to the end of the scene.

From the very first launch, audio demos play in English, French and Spanish. No sign-up, no email, no account: you hear the texture of the voice, the mood and the intensity levels before deciding anything.

When you start a tailor-made session, Caresse writes a scenario from your profile — who is taking part, which first names, what you're drawn to tonight, how long, what intensity, what mood. The voice names you. Above all, the text is composed to be spoken: pacing, pauses, breath. You close your eyes, there is nothing to read or type, and your hands stay free.

Nobody needs to hold the phone: the session plays on its own, which leaves both partners free — or lets you experience it entirely solo. Payment happens per session, with no subscription, and stays anonymous.

Bonded

A written chat, for planning ahead.

Bonded is built around Aria, an AI presented as an expert in BDSM practices. It asks what you want, takes into account the toys you own, assigns roles and rolls out the scene instruction by instruction, waiting for you to confirm before moving on. Add to that an archetype quiz, an intimacy profile, partner pairing and learning modules.

The limit shows up when it's time to act: you have to reply for the scene to move on. "Tell me what you're feeling", "tell me when you're ready" — every step waits for input. And you have to do it with your hands busy, often covered in lube: pick up the phone, unlock it, type. The rhythm drops. With a blindfold on, even solo, you have to take it off just to read the next step. The AI offers a workaround by addressing the partner, who reads the instructions and holds the phone; the scene continues, but one of you stays the operator. Solo, nobody can take that role: you are the one stopping at every step.

The first eleven exchanges are free, but an email address is requested before you can start. The model is subscription-based and image packs are billed separately. The interface is in English, but since this is a conversational AI, it replies without trouble in French or any other language.

Frequently asked questions

Caresse vs Bonded — your questions.

Can I try Caresse without giving my email?

Yes. Caresse offers audio demos that play from the first time you open the app, with no sign-up, no email and no account. Bonded asks for an email address before you can start the conversation with its AI, even for the 11 free exchanges.

What is the main difference between Caresse and Bonded?

When each one helps. Bonded works well beforehand: you talk with Aria, refine an idea, get explicit suggestions tailored to the toys you own. But its guidance waits for a written reply at every step, which becomes impractical once the scene has started. Caresse covers both moments: you set things up beforehand, then the audio session plays from start to finish with nobody having to reply.

Can you use Bonded mid-scene?

Not easily, and that is its main limitation. Every step waits for your reply before delivering the next: you have to pick the phone back up, hands often busy or covered in lube, and type. With a blindfold on, even solo, you have to take it off to read what comes next. As a couple, the AI offers a workaround by addressing the partner, who reads the instructions and holds the phone, but one of you then becomes the operator. Caresse was designed for this moment: once started, the session asks for nothing.

Does Bonded work in other languages?

Partly. The app interface is in English, but since Aria is a conversational AI, it replies fine in French or Spanish if you write to it in that language. The App Store listing only declares English. With Caresse everything is localized: interface, experiences and demos exist in English, French and Spanish, and the voices are produced in each language.

How much does Bonded cost compared to Caresse?

Bonded runs on subscriptions: the in-app purchases listed on the App Store range from $4.99 to $19.99, plus Aria message packs from $4.99 to $14.99. Caresse works per session, with no subscription: €1.99 for one experience, €5.49 for three, €7.99 for five.

Does Caresse include images like Bonded?

No. Caresse is an audio-only experience with no explicit visual content: everything rests on the voice and your imagination. Bonded offers paid image packs with its AI, on top of the subscription.

Can Bonded and Caresse be used together?

Yes. Bonded works beforehand, to explore ideas and ask questions. Caresse takes over when it's time to live it, when nobody wants to read or reply any more.

Are my sessions anonymous on Caresse?

Yes. Caresse runs on an anonymous identifier and a first name or pseudonym of your choice; no email is required, including for payment. Bonded advertises a “100% private” chat, but its App Store listing declares collection of name and phone number.
